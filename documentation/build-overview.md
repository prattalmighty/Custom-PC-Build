# Custom PC Build Overview

## Timeline & Background
* **Date Built:** Late 2020 (during the NVIDIA RTX 3000 series GPU launch window).
* **Motivation:** After receiving a severance package a few months prior, I had extra funds allocated for a major hardware upgrade. My older laptop was struggling to handle my photography editing workflow, and I wanted a powerful desktop that could handle both my creative business needs and personal gaming.

## Primary Purpose & Intended Use
* **Photo Editing:** Running demanding photo management and raw editing applications for my freelance photography business.
* **Gaming:** High-performance PC gaming ("double-dipping" on a single machine for work and play).

## Budget & Financial Considerations
* **Total Investment:** ~\$5,000 CAD.
* **Approach:** While I had a comfortable budget to spend, I wanted to balance high performance with practical value rather than spending without limits.

## Key Takeaways & Lessons Learned
* **Ease of Assembly:** Assembling the physical components was much more straightforward than expected—the process is surprisingly accessible once you understand component compatibility.
* **Regional Costs & Performance Scaling:** High-end hardware carries a steep premium, particularly in Canada. Achieving top-tier performance requires a significant investment, but investing in quality core components pays off in overall stability and longevity.
